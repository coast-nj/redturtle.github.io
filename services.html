Questions

Common questions, straight answers.

What does your monthly service plan include?

Routine inspections on your chosen schedule, photo reports of every visit, owner portal access, 24/7 emergency response line, and direct communication with your property manager. Specific work-order labor and materials are billed separately at clearly-disclosed rates.

Do you collect rent or manage tenants?

Not at this time. We focus on physical property care — inspections, maintenance, grounds, storm response, and HOA coordination. We do not currently handle rental income, leasing, or tenant placement. For those services we can refer you to licensed real estate brokerages we work alongside.

What's your typical response time for emergencies?

Under two hours, day or night. For active emergencies like burst pipes or storm damage, a crew is usually on-site within ninety minutes. Our 24/7 line is answered by a real person, every time.

Do you require long-term contracts?

For individual homeowners, no — our service agreements are month-to-month after the first quarter. We earn your business every month, and if it's not working, you're not locked in. HOA and condo association agreements are the exception: because board work involves budgeting, vendor commitments, and coordinated planning across an entire community, those engagements require a longer minimum term, typically one year. We'll walk your board through the specifics before anything is signed.

Can I still hire my own contractors?

Absolutely. Many owners have favorite plumbers or landscapers they want to keep. We coordinate with your preferred vendors and document the work just like our own crews.

Are your crews licensed and insured?

Yes. Our handyman team handles general maintenance directly, and we partner with licensed plumbers, electricians, and HVAC contractors for specialty work. Red Turtle carries $2M in general liability coverage, and all sub-trade partners must show current licensing and insurance before working on any property we manage.

Ready to hand off the headaches?

Tell us about your property and we'll come out for a free walkthrough — usually within the week.
