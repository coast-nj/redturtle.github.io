Questions

Common questions, straight answers.

What does your monthly service plan include?

Routine inspections on your chosen schedule, photo reports of every visit, owner portal access, 24/7 emergency response line, and direct communication with your property manager. Specific work-order labor and materials are billed separately at clearly-disclosed rates.

Do you collect rent and manage tenants?

Yes. With our New Jersey real estate license in place, we handle the full rental lifecycle — tenant screening, lease placement, rent collection with ACH deposits to owners, damage handling, and everything in between. This applies to long-term rentals and vacation rentals alike.

What kind of construction work do you do?

Repairs, updates, and renovations — both exterior (doors, windows, siding, decks) and interior (kitchens, baths, flooring, full remodels). We do not build custom homes from the ground up. If your project needs new construction of a residence, we can refer you to trusted local builders and stay involved to handle everything else.

Do you require long-term contracts?

For individual homeowners, no — our service agreements are month-to-month after the first quarter. We earn your business every month, and if it's not working, you're not locked in. HOA and condo association agreements are the exception: because board work involves budgeting, vendor commitments, and coordinated planning across an entire community, those engagements require a longer minimum term, typically one year. Rental management engagements also carry a season or lease-term minimum.

What's your typical response time for emergencies?

Under two hours, day or night. For active emergencies like burst pipes or storm damage, a crew is usually on-site within ninety minutes. Our 24/7 line is answered by a real person, every time.

Can I still hire my own contractors?

Absolutely. Many owners have favorite plumbers or landscapers they want to keep. We coordinate with your preferred vendors and document the work just like our own crews.

Are your crews licensed and insured?

Yes. Our handyman and construction crews handle general work directly, and we partner with licensed plumbers, electricians, and HVAC contractors for specialty trades. Red Turtle carries $2M in general liability coverage, holds a New Jersey real estate license, and all sub-trade partners must show current licensing and insurance before working on any property we manage.

Ready to hand off the headaches?

Tell us about your property and we'll come out for a free walkthrough — usually within the week.
