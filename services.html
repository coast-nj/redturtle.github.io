Questions

Straight answers.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

Individual homeowners are month-to-month — stay because the work is good, not because you’re locked in. HOA and condo association agreements do require a longer minimum term, typically one year, because board work involves budgeting, vendor commitments, and community-wide planning.

Do you collect rent?

No. We handle finding and placing the tenant — marketing, showings, screening, and the lease — but rent is paid directly to you. We don’t hold or disburse rent money on an owner’s behalf.

Do you build custom homes?

No. We focus on repairs, updates, and renovations — doors, windows, siding, decks, kitchens, baths, and interior work. For ground-up custom builds we’ll point you to someone who does it well.

What counts as an emergency, and how fast do you respond?

Water, heat, power, storm damage, or anything that puts the property or a tenant at risk. Our commitment is a two-hour response, and someone answers the line 24/7 in season.

How do I know what happened at my house?

Every visit comes with dated photos and a short written note. After storms you get a before-and-after set you can hand straight to your insurer.

Not sure which pillar you need?

Tell us what’s going on with the property. We’ll tell you honestly what it does and doesn’t need.
