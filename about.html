About

We live where you vacation.

Red Turtle is a locally owned property management and construction company based in Cape May County. Same crew in January as in July.

Why we started this

Most shore homes sit empty two-thirds of the year. The owners are an hour away, or five hours away, and the house is quietly dealing with salt air, wind, frozen pipes, and a pool that needs opening on a schedule nobody’s watching.

The usual options are a handyman who answers sometimes, or a management company big enough that your house is a row in a spreadsheet. We built Red Turtle to sit in between: a real crew, a real license, and a small enough book of properties that we know which house has the tricky outdoor shower valve.

The turtle isn’t decoration. A shell is a house you carry with you — slow, unglamorous, and extremely good at protecting what’s underneath.

If we wouldn’t leave our own house that way at the end of a visit, we don’t leave yours that way.
— How we settle every judgment call
Our rules

Three things we don’t bend on.

One

Answer the phone

A property manager you can’t reach isn’t managing anything. Someone picks up, and emergencies get a two-hour response.

Two

Show the work

Every visit comes with dated photos and a plain-language note. You should never have to ask what happened at your house.

Three

Quote it straight

One number, no padding, no surprise line items. If something turns out cheaper than expected, that’s what you pay.

How it goes

From first call to standing care.

  • Walkthrough

    We meet you at the property, top to bottom, and write down what it actually needs — not what we’d like to sell.

  • Plan & quote

    A written scope with pricing, split into what’s urgent, what’s seasonal, and what can wait.

  • Work begins

    Our crew handles what we do in-house and supervises the trades we don’t. You get photos as it goes.

  • Ongoing care

    Scheduled visits, seasonal prep, and a standing line for whatever comes up next.
