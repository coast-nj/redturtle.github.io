Our Story

One bad property manager started it all.

After watching too many shore owners get burned by absentee management companies — missed inspections, padded invoices, "we'll get there next week" excuses — we figured someone had to do this right.

That someone turned out to be us. Red Turtle started in a single Cape May Court House office with one truck, one phone, and a promise: answer every call, show up every time, charge only what's fair. Fifteen years later, we still answer every call.

We've grown to cover the full county, but the rules haven't changed. Every property gets the same attention as our first one did. Every owner talks to a real person. Every invoice tells you exactly what you paid for.

Today we do more than we did on day one. Property care is still the heart of the business, but we now handle full construction and renovations — from new doors and windows to full interior remodels — and, with our New Jersey real estate license, complete rental management including rent collection and financial reporting. One team, one point of contact, everything covered.

What We Believe

Three rules we don't break.

Every team gets a values poster on the wall. Ours actually drives the decisions we make every day.

No. 01

Answer Every Call

Real people, real phones. We don't route owners to chatbots, voicemail trees, or call centers in another time zone. When your property has a problem, you talk to someone who can fix it.

No. 02

Show Up Every Time

Scheduled inspections happen on schedule. After every storm, your property gets walked. Emergencies get response within hours. If we say we'll be there, we're there.

No. 03

Charge Only What's Fair

Flat fees clearly disclosed. Work orders itemized with labor and materials separate. Vendor markups capped and visible. You'll never wonder what you're paying for.
